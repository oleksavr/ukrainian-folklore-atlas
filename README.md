# Dyvosvit — Ukrainian Folklore Atlas

A small, bilingual, mobile-friendly folklore explorer, now with 22 figures and a Major Arcana card game. Search the atlas, filter figures by realm, switch between English and Ukrainian, open a short sourced story, draw a daily card, or lay a three-card past, present and future reading.

Play it at **https://oleksavr.github.io/ukrainian-folklore-atlas/**

On an iPhone, open that link in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen if you want it on your home screen.

## Run it

No build tools or dependencies are needed. Open `index.html` in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages link above.

The app is a static site, so it also works on any ordinary static host.

## The cards

The 22 Major Arcana are paired with Ukrainian folklore figures. The pairings are a modern reading for reflection and for fun, not a traditional village tarot, and not a claim about fate or the supernatural. A daily card stays the same until midnight (saved in the browser). Reversed meanings are optional. Your language choice is also remembered.

## Images

The creature pictures are realistic illustrations generated with Grok Imagine. They are imaginative depictions of folklore figures, not historical artworks or photographs of real beings. The home-screen icon is a crop of the Mavka illustration.

## Customize

All app content, styles, and interactions are in `index.html`. Creature summaries are paraphrased and linked to sources in the app. The collection emphasizes regional variation; it is an introduction to recorded traditions, not a claim that supernatural beings exist.
